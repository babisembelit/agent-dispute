🧪 Crypto Necessity Gut-Check

"What breaks if you remove the blockchain?"

  • Evidence immutability — Without on-chain storage, either party can tamper with logs. Blockchain makes evidence trustless.
  • Escrow enforcement — Smart contract escrow removes need for a trusted third party to hold funds.
  • Automated verdict execution — Verdict → fund release is atomic and can't be blocked by losing party.
  • Reputation portability — On-chain reputation is composable across all agent marketplaces.

Verdict: Crypto is essential. A centralized version would require trusting a single entity for escrow, evidence, and enforcement — defeating the purpose for autonomous agents.

This is a protocol-layer project — custom on-chain development is the right call. Integration-first applies to the periphery (tokens, RPCs, LLM APIs), not the core.
